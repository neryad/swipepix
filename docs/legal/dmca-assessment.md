# SwipePix DMCA Assessment

Last reviewed: 2026-09-25

## Current status

DMCA designated-agent registration does not appear necessary for the current app because SwipePix does not provide user accounts, public posting, cloud upload, remote storage, sharing, or hosting of user content. The app reviews photos locally on the user’s device and requests deletion through the operating system.

## Reopen this assessment if any feature adds

- Cloud backup or sync of photos.
- User accounts with uploaded content.
- Shared albums or public links.
- Community, feed, comments, profiles, or public galleries.
- Server-side image analysis, thumbnailing, or storage.
- Any web app where users can upload images, videos, audio, or files.

## If DMCA becomes applicable

Before launch of any hosting/upload feature:

1. Register a designated DMCA agent with the U.S. Copyright Office.
2. Publish the agent’s contact information in a public Terms/DMCA page.
3. Add a takedown and counter-notice process to Terms.
4. Adopt and implement a repeat-infringer termination policy.
5. Create a renewal reminder before the three-year expiration.

## Draft takedown clause if needed later

DMCA / Copyright Policy. We respect intellectual property rights. If you believe content hosted by SwipePix infringes your copyright, send a notice to our Designated Agent including: (1) your physical or electronic signature; (2) identification of the copyrighted work; (3) identification of the allegedly infringing material and its location; (4) your contact information; (5) a statement of good-faith belief that use is unauthorized; and (6) a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act. Designated Agent: [Name], [Email], [Address]. We will remove or disable access to infringing material promptly upon a valid notice and may terminate repeat infringers.
